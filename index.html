Questions we often hear

Which platforms do you build for?

Android, iOS and the web. We can build one app for both phone platforms with Flutter, or separate native apps if your project needs it.

Can you publish the app for me?

Yes. We prepare the store listing, screenshots, icons and policy details, and can publish from your developer account or guide you through it.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the features. A simple app or website can take a few weeks, while larger apps and games take longer. We give you an estimate after the first discussion.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We fix bugs, update the app for new Android and iOS versions, and add features when you are ready.
